ETERNAL NOTE
Permanent notes that earn — by @okfreelancer

WHAT IT IS
A single-file dApp that lets anyone publish a permanent note on Arweave.
Every note is tippable. Tips and AI-agent payments (x402-ready) go directly to the creator’s wallet.

WHY IT SELLS
• Permanent storage (Arweave) — no servers, no link rot
• Direct earnings — no middleman, no platform cut on tips
• Agentic ready — notes tagged for x402 / AI agent discovery
• One-click publish with Wander / ArConnect
• Ownership locked to @okfreelancer branding

HOW CREATORS MAKE MONEY
1. Connect their Arweave wallet
2. Write & post a note
3. Share the permanent link
4. Receive tips / agent payments straight to their wallet
5. Withdraw anytime inside Wander or ArConnect

POSITIONING
“Post once. Live forever. Get tipped by humans and AI agents.”

TARGET
Independent creators, AI agent developers, Permaweb builders.
